# Letters from the Practice: house style

Approved by Niki on 2026-11-01 ("literally perfect, keep this style"). Use it for every monthly letter until she changes it.
The November 2026 letter is the reference build: `2026-11_letters-from-the-practice.html`, generated by `build_nov.py`.
For a new month, copy `build_nov.py`, change the copy and images, and run it.

## Layout

- Every section sits on its dark brand color, full width. Content goes in light cards inside it.
- All text is left-aligned, including the opening letter.
- Each brand section opens with its logo on a small cream square, then the section name in small caps.
- Every item gets an image from the practice's own Shopify Files. No stock photos and no generated clinicians or offices.
- Links sit behind words ("Click here," "Read it," "Start with Part One"). Never print the URL after the link.

## Content rules

- The letter goes to clients and colleagues alike. Do not promote the memoir (The Stories We Told) in it. The blog and the essays are fine.
- Open with "Read the part that is yours and skip the rest."
- Sign the letter "Niki."
- Use "New this month" instead of exact publish dates, so the letter does not go stale.
- Any health tip carries the general-education line, plus specific cautions where they apply (for example, light exposure and bipolar disorder).
- End with "If you are in crisis, call or text 988."
- No em dashes or en dashes. American English.

## Subject and preview text

- Subject: `Letters from the Practice | <Month>`
- Preview text: one plain sentence naming the two or three things a reader most needs, the practical item first. Example: "A new client portal, holiday schedules, and a new website."

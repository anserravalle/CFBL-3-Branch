A Note to Our Growing Community

Our community is growing,
and there’s a place in it
for you

 

Center for Balanced Living has always been more than a therapy room. It’s a community of people learning to live, heal, and grow well together. As that community grows, so does the work, and today we want to welcome you into all three parts of it. Wherever you are on your path, you belong here.

Three Ways to Belong

Our community gathers in three divisions: care for those who are healing, learning for the professionals who guide them, and words for anyone walking the path. Different doors, the same table. Here’s where you fit.

Division 01  ·  For Clients

Clinical Services

Trauma-informed, relational care for individuals and families, grounded in the understanding that healing happens in relationship.

• Individual Therapy
Relational, trauma-informed therapy that moves beyond symptom management.
• Psychological Evaluations
Comprehensive ADHD, autism & neurodevelopmental, and psychological assessments.
• Wellness & Support Groups
Guided, supported spaces, from art therapy to grief and pet-loss groups.

Division 02  ·  For Professionals

The CFBL Institute

The educational branch of our community, offering clinically thoughtful learning for therapists, helping professionals, and curious minds.

• Professional Trainings
Advanced, CE-eligible trainings for licensed clinicians and graduate students.
• Workshops
Research-informed sessions on trauma recovery and nervous-system regulation.
• Courses & Certification
Self-paced online courses and certification programs in trauma-informed practice.

Musa

• Books
Long-form writing on trauma, healing, and the work of living well.
• Essays & Publications
Reflections and articles on psychology, the nervous system, and meaning.
• Letters From the Practice

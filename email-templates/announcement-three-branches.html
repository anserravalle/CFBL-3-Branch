A Note to Our Growing Community

Our community is growing,
and there’s a place in it
for you

 

Center for Balanced Living has always been more than a therapy room — it’s a community of people learning to live, heal, and grow well together. As that community grows, so does the work, and today we want to welcome you into all three corners of it. Wherever you are on your path, you belong here.

Three Ways to Belong

Our community gathers in three ways — for those seeking care, for professionals deepening their craft, and for clinicians building a meaningful career. Different doors, the same table. Here’s where you fit.

Branch 01  ·  For Clients

The Clinical Practice

Trauma-informed, relational care for individuals and families — grounded in the understanding that healing happens in relationship, and that trauma lives in the nervous system.

— Individual Therapy
Relational, trauma-informed therapy that moves beyond symptom management.
— Psychological Evaluations
Comprehensive ADHD, autism & neurodevelopmental, and psychological assessments.
— Wellness & Support Groups
Guided, supported spaces — from art therapy to grief and pet-loss groups.

Branch 02  ·  For Professionals

The CFBL Institute

The educational and professional-development branch of our practice — clinically thoughtful, interdisciplinary learning for therapists, helping professionals, and curious minds.

— Professional Trainings
Advanced, CE-eligible trainings for licensed clinicians and graduate students.
— Workshops
Research-informed sessions on trauma recovery and nervous-system regulation.
— Courses & Certification
Self-paced online courses and certification programs in trauma-informed practice.

Branch 03  ·  For Clinicians

Career Development

Professional pathways for clinicians who value thoughtful care, continued growth, and meaningful service — from licensure support to a place on our team.

— Clinical Supervision
Trauma-informed supervision for LPC associates, PsyD trainees & licensed clinicians.
— EMDR Consultation
Consultation toward EMDR certification and deeper clinical confidence.
— Join Our Team
Build your career inside a practice that prizes thoughtful, sustainable care.

Regenerate it in the same AI image tool that made the original, using the prompt below,

## Settings
- **No text / no lettering** in the image (the title is added afterward, crisp).
- **Resolution:** as large as the tool allows — at least **1875 × 2775 px**; 2048×3072 or 4K is better.
- **Aspect ratio:** 2:3 vertical (portrait). Generate a few variations and pick the closest.
- Leave the **upper third smoky and open** (room for the title) and the **lower-right darker**.

## Prompt (paste this)
> Somber, painterly literary book-cover artwork, vertical composition: a woman with her hair
> in a loose low updo tilting her head back with eyes closed, and a man standing close behind
> and beside her seen in profile — both figures half-dissolving into billowing pale gray storm
> clouds and smoke. A tangle of fine blood-red threads (the red thread of fate) crisscrossing
> and weaving across the entire image. Muted, desaturated palette of warm gray, pale stone, and
> deep oxblood red. Cinematic, melancholic, emotionally heavy; the figures semi-transparent,
> merging into the smoke at the edges; soft directional light; a faint vertical fracture down
> the center. Generous open smoky sky at the top. No text, no words, no lettering, no watermark,
> no signature.

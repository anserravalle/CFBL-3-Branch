This policy covers registrations and purchases made on drserravalle.com. Programs are offered through CFBL Institute and taught by Dr. Niki Serravalle, PsyD, LPCMH, NCC.

Live programs: courses, workshops, and trainings

A participant who cancels in writing at least 7 days before the program date receives a full refund.

A participant who cancels fewer than 7 days before the program date may transfer once, at no charge, to a future offering of the same program, or receive a refund less a 10 percent administrative fee.

No refund is issued after the program begins.

If a program is canceled or rescheduled

If CFBL Institute cancels or reschedules a program, registered participants are notified in writing and offered a full refund or a transfer to a future offering at no additional cost. CFBL Institute is not responsible for other costs participants incur in anticipation of a canceled program.

EMDR consultation and clinical supervision

Refund requests for consultation sessions and packages are reviewed individually. Write to the address below before your next scheduled session. Clinical supervision terms are set out in the written supervision agreement signed before supervision begins.

How to request a refund or transfer

Send your request in writing to drserravalle@gmail.com with your name, the program, and its date. Requests are processed within 14 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may need additional time to post the refund.

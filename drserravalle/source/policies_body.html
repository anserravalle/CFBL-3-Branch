These policies apply to all CFBL Institute live continuing education programs. Questions, requests, and grievances go to the ACEP Administrator at drserravalle@gmail.com or (302) 608-3780.

Program Fees, Refunds, and Cancellation

Fees. The fee for each program is published on its registration page before registration opens. Fees include live instruction, program materials, and a certificate of completion for verified attendance.

Refunds. A participant who cancels in writing at least 7 days before the program date receives a full refund. A participant who cancels fewer than 7 days before the program date may transfer once, at no charge, to a future offering of the same program, or receive a refund less a 10 percent administrative fee. No refund is issued after the program begins.

Requests. Refund and transfer requests are submitted in writing to the ACEP Administrator and processed within 14 business days.

Cancellation by CFBL Institute. If CFBL Institute cancels or reschedules a program, registered participants are notified in writing and offered a full refund or a transfer to a future offering at no additional cost. CFBL Institute is not responsible for other costs participants incur in anticipation of a canceled program.

Participant Complaints and Grievances

Participants may submit a written grievance about program content, instruction, accessibility, or administration to the ACEP Administrator at drserravalle@gmail.com. A grievance should identify the program and date, describe the concern, and state the resolution requested.

The ACEP Administrator acknowledges receipt in writing within 5 business days. The Program Administrator reviews the grievance and provides a written response within 14 business days of receipt describing the review, findings, and any action taken. A participant who is not satisfied with the resolution may contact NBCC at continuinged@nbcc.org. CFBL Institute does not retaliate against anyone who submits a grievance. Grievance records are retained for 5 years.

Confidentiality of Participant Information

CFBL Institute collects only the information needed to administer programs, meet recordkeeping requirements, and communicate with participants about programs and certificates. Payment card data is processed by Shopify and is not stored by CFBL Institute. Participant information is stored in access-restricted systems, is not sold or shared for marketing, and is not used outside program administration without consent. Participants may request a copy of, or a correction to, their information by emailing the ACEP Administrator.

Attendance and Certificates

Attendance at live programs is verified with the registration roster, the Zoom attendance report or sign-in sheet, and in-session check-ins. Credit is awarded for verified instructional time attended, in quarter-hour increments; breaks, meals, and introductions are not counted. Certificates are issued after the program ends and are emailed within 7 business days. Replacement certificates are available at no cost for 5 years.

Accessibility

CFBL Institute provides accessible programs consistent with the Americans with Disabilities Act. Live Zoom sessions offer captioning. Participants may request accommodations at registration or at any time by contacting the ACEP Administrator; requests receive a response within 5 business days.
